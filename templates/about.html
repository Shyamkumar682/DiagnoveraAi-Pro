Behind the Neural Engine

A transparent look into how DiagnoveraAI processes symptoms, synthesizes algorithms, and predicts potential conditions using advanced machine learning.

1. Data Synthesis

The system ingests a highly structured matrix of symptoms mapped to specific diseases, utilizing one-hot encoding. Noise and outliers are programmatically synthesized out to ensure the training data is both robust and clinically valid.

2. Algorithm Training

Our framework trains a dual-model ensemble, pitting Decision Trees against Random Forests. It applies rigorous hyperparameter tuning (GridSearchCV) to locate the absolute best constraints, pruning the forest to mitigate overfitting.

3. Real-time Inference

When you select your symptoms, our decoupled Model Engine instantly serializes your input against the loaded neural footprint and returns a calculated probabilistic diagnosis and matching care precautions in milliseconds.

4. X-Ray Image Processing

When diagnosing Respiratory issues like Pneumonia, our engine uses a Deep Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) built on TensorFlow. It translates your X-Ray scan into pixel vectors, shrinks dimensionality using max pooling, and classifies the pathology with absolute confidence using sigmoid activations.
