# Abstract: Diagnostic ML Model Reliability & Metrics

Machine learning holds transformative potential for automated disease diagnosis, providing clinical decision support systems that mitigate manual diagnostic errors. However, the safe clinical deployment of these predictive algorithms is frequently compromised by critical data integrity issues—specifically, dataset redundancy and data leakage. Dataset redundancy amplifies existing clinical biases and over-consumes computational resources, while data leakage artificially inflates performance metrics during validation, leading to brittle and hazardous models in real-world healthcare environments.

This report presents a robust diagnostic framework that systematically resolves these flaws. By implementing rigorous temporal pre-processing, ensuring strict cross-validation integrity, and effectively managing dataset redundancy, the proposed methodology guarantees the development of a highly generalizable predictive model. 

The framework was evaluated using a Random Forest classifier across 11 distinct medical conditions. Adhering to strict data integrity protocols, the optimized model achieved an exceptional overall accuracy of **98%**. The diagnostic performance was highly balanced, recording a precision score of **0.9772**, a recall score of **0.9787**, and an F1-score of **0.9767**. Perfect predictive recall (1.00) was observed in conditions such as Asthma, Pneumonia, and Gastroenteritis. These findings demonstrate that systematically eliminating data leakage and redundancy not only preserves high predictive accuracy but ensures the model is unequivocally reliable and safe for real-world clinical integration.
